On our websites www.ikea.com, www.inter.ikea.com, www.ikeamuseum.com, www.jobs.ikea.com, and www.ikeasocialentrepreneurship.org we use cookies. These are small text files that are stored on your computer, phone or tablet. In addition to cookies, there are other technologies that we use to store and read information on your computer, tablet or mobile phone, such as Javascript, tracking pixels and local storage. For the sake of convenience we will also refer to them as 'cookies'.

Read more about the categories of cookies that we use on our websites below. The actual cookies placed may differ per website. You can find detailed Information about the cookies placed on each page, either in the cookie banner, or at the bottom of the page under “Cookie settings”.

Types of cookies used on our websites

a.    Functional cookies

Our websites www.ikea.com, www.inter.ikea.com, www.ikeamuseum.com, www.jobs.ikea.com, www.ikeasocialentrepreneurship.org all use functional cookies. Functional cookies are necessary for our websites to work properly. For example, these enable you to easily navigate through our websites and to register your privacy settings, view videos and remember your language and country preferences. These cookies are always set and we do not require your consent to use them.

For this we use unique identifiers such as your IP-address. We base the processing of data through these cookies on our legitimate interest.

b.    Preference cookies

Our website www.jobs.ikea.com uses preference cookies. These cookies help us display relevant content based on how you interact with our site -for example, which job vacancies you’ve viewed. They also allow you to save vacancies that interest you. To do this, we use unique identifiers such as your IP address and device ID. We base the processing of data through these cookies on your consent.

c.    Analytical cookies

Our websites www.ikea.com, www.inter.ikea.com, www.ikeamuseum.com, www.jobs.ikea.com, www.ikeasocialentrepreneurship.org all use analytical cookies.

Analytical cookies help us enhance our websites and services, and provide you with a more personalized experience by tailoring content to your individual interests.

Together with our partners, we use these cookies to collect information about how you interact with our websites—such as time spent, pages visited, geolocation, audience size,  usage patterns, and video usage . This involves the use of unique identifiers, including your IP address, device information, and internet connection details, for system administration purposes.

To safeguard your privacy, we have agreed with our partners on privacy-friendly settings. These include anonymizing your IP address, limiting further data processing, and disabling tracking.

We base the placement of analytical cookies and the processing of data collected through these cookies on your consent. If you do not accept analytical cookies it may adversely affect your experience of the website.

d.    Marketing cookies

On our websites www.ikeasocialentrepreneurship.org and www.jobs.ikea.com, we use marketing cookies. We and our partners use these cookies to track visitors across websites with the purpose to display advertisements tailored to your interests so that they are as relevant to you as possible. For this we use unique identifiers such as your IP address, your computer and internet and system administration.

We base the placement of marketing cookies and the processing of data collected through these cookies on your consent.  If you do not accept marketing cookies it may adversely affect your experience of the website.

Detailed Information about our cookies and how to manage your cookies

You can find detailed information about the cookies that are placed and the partners we use by clicking on the “cookie settings” link that is shown at the bottom of each website. There you can also withdraw your consent anytime and change your cookie preferences.

When you visit our IKEA store in Delft (EN)

In this chapter we describe how we process your personal information in our IKEA store in Delft. Please note that all other stores in the Netherlands and the website www.ikea.com/nl are owned by our franchisee IKEA BV. Check their privacy statement on www.ikea.com/nl to read more about how IKEA BV processes your personal information.

1.        Purchase in the store

When you buy a product or service at our IKEA store in Delft, we record the transaction so we can complete your order. If you scan your IKEA FAMILY card, we link the purchase to your account. This allows you to benefit from advantages that come with being an IKEA FAMILY member.

If you use one of our services -like kitchen installation, wardrobe assembly, or home delivery- we collect your address, email address, and phone number. We need this information to schedule appointments and keep you informed. Sometimes we share your details with service providers who carry out the work for us, such as delivery companies. They only receive the information they need to do the job and are not allowed to use it for anything else or share it with others.

If you use a means of transport through our store, such as a trailer, this is arranged via our partner Freetrailer. You provide your personal information directly to them, and we do not receive any of it. For more details on how Freetrailer handles your data, please visit their website.

We use your personal data to complete your purchase, provide services, communicate with you about appointments and to improve our store and our services. We base our processing primarily on the legal ground of performance of an agreement. When we use your data to improve our store and our services, we do so based on our legitimate interest.

We keep your purchase data for seven years to comply with tax laws. Data we use to analyze shopping behavior is kept for two years. Information related to services provided is kept only as long as needed to provide the service and follow up if necessary.

2.        IKEA Product Planner

Before making a purchase, you may want to use one of our planning tools. Some of these tools allow you to save your design and generate a unique code to retrieve it later. This process is entirely anonymous.

In certain planning tools, you also have the option to email the code to yourself, so you don’t need to remember it. If you choose this option, we will temporarily store your email address solely for the purpose of sending you the code. We retain your email address for one week, after which it is automatically deleted. This processing is based on our legitimate interest

3.        IKEA FAMILY

IKEA FAMILY is managed by IKEA BV, which operates all IKEA stores in the Netherlands -except for the Delft store, which we own- as well as the website www.ikea.com/nl.

When you sign up for IKEA FAMILY, IKEA BV collects your name, date of birth, email address, username, password, phone number (optional), postal code, and preferred store. They also collect information about your purchases when you scan your card in-store. This data is used to manage the IKEA FAMILY program and provide you with relevant rewards. For more information about how IKEA BV handles your personal data, please refer to their privacy statement on www.ikea.com/nl.

If you live in the catchment area of our Delft store or have selected Delft as your preferred store, we may use your personal data to better understand the needs and preferences of our local customers. This helps us improve your shopping experience in our Delft store. If you gave your consent when signing up, we may also send you newsletters with local offers and updates -such as new products, special promotions, or inspiring workshops.

We process your personal data for these purposes based on our legitimate interest in improving our store and services. However, we will only send you local offers if you have given your consent.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by logging into your account on www.ikea.com/nl and updating your preferences, or by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter.

4.        Camera security

All our store is equipped with cameras. These are needed for your safety, for the safety of our staff, and to protect our products. We base the processing of your personal information on the legal ground of our legitimate interest.

Camera images will be deleted after four weeks unless there are good reasons for storing them for longer, for example, as part of a police investigation.

5.        Using our wifi network

You can use our wifi network for free when you visit our store. Once you are logged on to our wifi network some data, such as the identification number (MAC address) of your device, is automatically collected. We base the processing of your personal information on the legal ground of our legitimate interest.

This data will be deleted after one year and IKEA only uses it for preventing any misuse. If any illegal activities are conducted from our wifi network we may block the access of the device by which you use our wifi network.

6.        Playing in Småland

For your child to be able to play at Småland we need some information from you. For example, we need your name and telephone number, the name and age of your child, and a photo of you and your child. This way we can ensure that a child is collected by the right person and we are able to contact you if anything happens to your child. We will ask your permission to take a photo. We base the processing of your personal information on the legal ground of your consent.

13.  Jouw privacyrechten

Je hebt verschillende privacyrechten:

Recht op inzage: Je kunt ons vragen of wij jouw persoonsgegevens verwerken. Als dat zo is, kun je een kopie van deze gegevens opvragen.

Recht op rectificatie: Als je denkt dat jouw persoonsgegevens onjuist of onvolledig zijn, kun je ons vragen deze te corrigeren of aan te vullen.

Recht van bezwaar: Je kunt op elk moment bezwaar maken tegen de verwerking van jouw gegevens. Als wij geen dwingende, gerechtvaardigde redenen kunnen aantonen om de verwerking voort te zetten, moeten wij deze stoppen.

Recht op beperking van verwerking: In bepaalde gevallen, bijvoorbeeld als je bezwaar hebt gemaakt, kun je vragen om de verwerking van jouw gegevens tijdelijk te beperken.

Recht op verwijdering ("recht om vergeten te worden"): In sommige gevallen kun je vragen om jouw persoonsgegevens te laten verwijderen. Als wij jouw gegevens nodig hebben om te voldoen aan wettelijke verplichtingen of om een overeenkomst met jou uit te voeren, kunnen we ze niet verwijderen.

Recht op overdraagbaarheid van gegevens: Als wij jouw gegevens verwerken op basis van een overeenkomst, kun je in bepaalde gevallen vragen om jouw gegevens te ontvangen zodat je ze elders kunt gebruiken, bijvoorbeeld om ze over te dragen aan een andere organisatie.

Ben je het niet eens met hoe wij jouw persoonsgegevens verwerken? Dan kun je een privacyklacht bij ons indienen zoals hieronder beschreven, of een klacht indienen bij de nationale toezichthouder.

Onze websites zijn niet gericht op kinderen en we hebben geen kennis van het feit dat we persoonsgegevens van kinderen verkopen of delen. Als je weet dat een kind persoonsgegevens aan ons heeft verstrekt, vragen we je contact met ons op te nemen via een van de onderstaande methoden zodat we deze gegevens kunnen verwijderen.

Wil je jouw rechten uitoefenen of meer informatie ontvangen? Neem gerust contact met ons op. Het uitoefenen van jouw rechten heeft geen invloed op hoe wij jou behandelen.

Your privacy rights

You have a number of privacy rights:

Right of access. You can ask us whether we process your personal information and if so receive a copy of such data.

Right to rectification. If you believe that the personal information relating to you is inaccurate or incomplete, you can request to have the data rectified or completed.

Right of objection. You can object to the processing at any time. If we cannot show that there are compelling, authorised reasons to continue processing the data, we must terminate the processing.

Right to limitation of processing. In certain cases, for example if you have objected to the processing, you have the possibility to ask that the processing of your personal information be limited.

Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"). You can in certain cases have your personal information erased. When your personal data is needed for us, for example to meet legal obligations or to perform an agreement with you, we cannot erase your data.

Right to data portability. If we processes personal data relating to you to perform an agreement, you have the possibility in certain cases to be given the personal data relating to you to use it somewhere else, for example transfer the data to another data controller.
