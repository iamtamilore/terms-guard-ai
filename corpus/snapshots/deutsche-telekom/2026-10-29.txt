Privacy Notice

What data do we collect, how is it used, and how long is it stored?

a) Required processing for providing the digital service (Art. 6 para. 1b GDPR, §25 para. 2 no. 2 TDDDG) 

When you use our website telekom.com, hereinafter referred to as the digital service:

When you visit our web pages, the web server temporarily records the domain name or IP address of your computer, the client's file request (file name and URL), the http response code, and the website from which you visit us. The logged data is used exclusively for data security purposes, in particular to defend against attempted attacks on our web server (Art. 6 para. 1f GDPR). This data is not used to create individual user profiles, nor is it shared with third parties, and it is deleted after a maximum of seven days. We reserve the right to statistically evaluate anonymized data sets.

b) Additional processing for providing the digital service, carried out on the basis of your consent, e.g., optional communication channels (Art. 6 para. 1a GDPR, §25 para. 1 TDDDG)

Web forms: We do not collect additional personal data such as your name, address, telephone number, or email address unless you provide this information voluntarily—for example, when you subscribe to our newsletter. For more information about newsletters, see section 1e).

 

c) Processing activities for providing the digital service that are based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, §25(2)(2) TDDDG)

When you use our digital service, our servers temporarily log the domain name or IP address of your device, along with other data such as the requested content or response code. 

We use the logged data solely for data security purposes, especially to protect our servers from attempted attacks. We do not use this data to create individual user profiles, nor do we share it with third parties. All data is deleted after no more than 7 days. We reserve the right to perform statistical analysis on anonymized data sets.

 

d) Processing performed by third parties when providing the digital service

Many operating systems offer the option to dictate text in addition to entering it via keyboard. When you use this feature, your speech is processed by the third party (for example, Apple, Google, or Microsoft) as the controller, and the result is displayed in the input field. For details about how this feature works, how you can enable or disable it, and the legal basis for processing, please refer to the relevant operating system provider (third party).

 

e) Privacy notice of Deutsche Telekom AG for newsletter delivery on telekom.com.

The General Privacy Notice of Deutsche Telekom AG applies to the processing of your personal data. 

If you use and/or subscribe to our newsletters, the following additional information also applies.

The privacy notice for newsletter delivery informs you which personal data is collected for newsletter delivery, for what purposes this data is processed, when this data is deleted, and on what legal basis this takes place.

 

What data is collected and for what purpose is it processed? 

If you sign up for a newsletter, we use your email address to send you the respective newsletter, which keeps you regularly informed about topics related to Deutsche Telekom AG. The legal basis is your consent in accordance with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. To ensure your newsletter registration is valid—that is, to prevent unauthorized sign-ups on behalf of others—we will send you a confirmation email as part of the double opt-in process after your initial registration, asking you to confirm your subscription.

In connection with your newsletter registration, we also store your registration data (such as email address, date, and time of registration/confirmation of opt-in) so that we can later verify and prove your registration if needed. The legal basis for this storage is our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f), Art. 7(1) GDPR. This legitimate interest is based on the need to provide proof of your consent.

How long do we keep your data?

We store your email address so we can send you the newsletter, until you unsubscribe or we stop sending the newsletter to you. After you unsubscribe, your registration data will be kept—solely for documentation purposes—for up to 3 ½ years.

How can I withdraw from receiving the newsletter?

You can withdraw your consent to receive newsletters from Deutsche Telekom AG at any time with effect for the future by clicking the unsubscribe link at the end of any newsletter you receive.

Data control for social media plug-ins and links to social media platforms

Some pages include buttons from social media networks (such as Facebook, Google, Instagram, X [Twitter], Pinterest, Xing, TikTok, or LinkedIn) that let you recommend the offerings on the corporate website telekom.com to your friends and contacts. 

To ensure you have full control over your data, these buttons only create a direct connection between the social network and you as a visitor once you actively click the button (one-click solution).

In our digital service, we only use the pictograms of each social media network. You will only be redirected to our company page on the respective social media platform after clicking on the pictogram. The social media platforms and third-party content providers that can be accessed via these pictograms provide their services and process your data under their own responsibility.  

By activating the social media plug-in or link through the pictogram, including for sharing content (Art. 6 para. 1 a GDPR), the following data may be transmitted to the social media providers: IP address, browser information, operating system, screen resolution, installed browser plug-ins, previous page if you followed a link (referrer), the URL of the current page, and so on.

The next time you visit, the social media plug-ins will again be provided in the default inactive mode, ensuring that no data is transmitted on your next visit. 

You can find more information about social media plug-ins, the scope and purposes of the respective data processing, as well as additional privacy-related information in the privacy policies of each provider and in the explanation of the  <<1-Click Solution on Heise.de>>.

What rights do I have?

You have the right to

a) to request information about the categories of data processed, the purposes of processing, any recipients of the data, and the planned retention period (Art. 15 GDPR);

b) to request the correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete data (Art. 16 GDPR); 

c) to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 para. 3 GDPR);

d) to object to data processing that is based on legitimate interests, at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation (Art. 21 para. 1 GDPR), by stating those reasons. You may object to data processing for direct marketing purposes at any time without stating any reasons (Art. 21 paras. 2, 3 GDPR);

e) in certain cases under Art. 17 GDPR to request the erasure of data—especially if the data is no longer needed for its intended purpose or is being processed unlawfully, or if you have withdrawn your consent as described above (c) or have objected as described above (d); 

f) under certain conditions, to request the restriction of data processing if erasure is not possible or the obligation to erase is disputed (Art. 18 GDPR);

g) to data portability, meaning you can receive your data that you have provided to us in a commonly used, machine-readable format, such as CSV, and, if applicable, transmit it to others (Art. 20 GDPR);

h) to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority regarding data processing (for telecommunications contracts: Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information; otherwise: State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia).
